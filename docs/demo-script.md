# Wander — 90-second hackathon demo

Record in Cap with the app filling the screen and your camera in a small corner. Aim to finish in 88 seconds, leaving two seconds of buffer.

## Structure and script

| Time | Beat | Spoken script | On-screen action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–15s | The problem and Wander | “On holiday, we wanted a guide we could ask questions. So we built Wander, with voice and interactive 3D. Here’s Tower Bridge.” | Have Tower Bridge open from the first frame. Click **Start your tour** as you finish “Tower Bridge.” |
| 15–23s | Start the experience | Stay quiet and let the actual guide speak. | Let viewers hear its introduction. |
| 23–56s | The main demo moment | To Wander: “Wait—can you show me how the bridge opens?” Then stay quiet for the response. | Interrupt the guide. Let its real answer and the bridge-opening animation play. Move on when the response finishes; this window includes room for live response delays. |
| 56–66s | Explain the value | “You can follow your curiosity, ask questions, and see how things work—at your own pace.” | Click **Pause guide** before addressing the viewer again. Leave the bridge visible. |
| 66–81s | Codex’s contribution | “Codex helped us build the Three.js bridge, animate its mechanisms, and connect OpenAI’s voice guide to tools that move the camera, highlight details, and demonstrate how the bridge works.” | Keep the app onscreen. Gently orbit the model if useful. |
| 81–88s | Close | “We started with Tower Bridge. We want exploring a place to feel like having a guide beside you.” | Settle on a clear view of the whole bridge and finish looking at the camera. |
| 88–90s | Buffer | No additional narration. | Hold the final view. |

## Recording notes

- Rehearse the exact interruption. The guide’s response is live; the timing above reserves space for it rather than prescribing its words.
- Aim to start the tour at 15 seconds. The opening is 25 words; rehearse it aloud and allow up to 18 seconds at a relaxed pace. Skip any extra greeting or explanation before clicking.
- Pause the guide before talking to the viewer again so it does not respond to the presentation.
- Test that Cap captures both your microphone and the app’s audio. Use headphones to avoid feedback.
- Keep the webcam overlay away from the bridge and tour controls.
- Keep the map and additional features out of this cut. Demonstrate one question clearly.
- Say “OpenAI’s voice guide” without naming a specific model unless the version being recorded has been verified.
- Record the working local app and keep credentials and private event details offscreen.
